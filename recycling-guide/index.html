    By Material

    Air Conditioners

    • 2 Rivers Industries, Inc. — Hannibal, MO, (573) 221-3211
    • Frank's Appliance Heating & Air Conditioning — 105 W Clay St, Edina, MO 63537, (660) 397-2241 ✅ Confirmed active. Run by Frank Crabtree Sr.; can repair or dispose depending on brand & BTU.

    Bike Tubes & Event Tents

    • Green Guru Gear — mail-in upcycling program for bike tubes (no Slime/thorn-resistant tubes or tires) and event tents. Not yet independently re-verified.
    • Alchemy Goods — mail-in tube upcycling. Not yet independently re-verified.

    Batteries

    • Menard's (Kirksville) — all battery types, including tool batteries
    • Interstate All Battery Center — 101 48th St, Quincy, IL 62305, (217) 214-1069. Bring a blank check from Cattail — fee per pound for certain battery types. Not yet independently re-verified.
    • Batteries Plus Bulbs — 805 E. Nifong Blvd, Columbia, MO 65201, (573) 397-7495. All batteries (rechargeable or not), all bulb types; fee for non-LED bulbs and non-rechargeable batteries. Not yet independently re-verified.

    Christmas Lights

    • Mail-in: christmas-light-source.com recycling program. Not yet independently re-verified.

    Clothing / Textiles

    • The Crossing Thrift Store — 1105 S Baltimore St, Kirksville, MO 63501, (660) 956-0587. Donations Mon/Wed/Fri 10am–5:30pm.
    • Salvation Army — Quincy (425 Broadway St, (217) 224-5998) and Kirksville (420 S Baltimore St, (660) 665-4326). Resale + recycling via Remains program partner.

    Electronic Recycling

    • Anderson Computer Consultants LLC — see Kirksville table above. ✅ Confirmed.
    • Goodwill — 3904 Broadway St, Quincy, IL 62305, (217) 779-7701. All electronics except TVs.
    • 2 Rivers Industries, Inc. — see Hannibal table above. ✅ Confirmed.
    • Greendisk (mail-in) — CDs, tapes, cords, mice, phones, cartridges.
    • Amazon Recycling Program (mail-in) — devices & accessories, prepaid shipping.

    Inkjet Cartridges

    • Free mail-in recycling, envelopes requestable online.

    Light Bulbs

    • Menard's (Kirksville/Quincy) — most bulb types.
    • Home Depot — 3015 N Baltimore St, Kirksville, MO 63501, (660) 627-2777. LED, fluorescent, CFL — bring to self-checkout and ask for bulb recycling.

    Scrap Metal

    • Adam Dannenhauer — (660) 341-9926 (cell), based in Azen, MO. Will haul scrap metal/large trash from Dancing Rabbit for free (paid via the metal recycling facility). No tires. Not yet independently re-verified.

    Televisions

    • Dean Steinkamp Clean Up Jobs (previously listed as "Steinkamp Hauling") — 2833 N 12th Street, Quincy, IL, (217) 224-3390. Mon–Fri 8am–3:30pm, $1/screen inch. ✅ Confirmed active as of July 2026. Note: "Steinkamp Hauling, L.L.C." is a different, unrelated-in-service residential trash-collection company under the same family — don't confuse the two; use this exact name/number for TV drop-off.

# Mask

Recycling has a new mask for crushing glass! It's a 3M Half Face Silicone Respirator Mask with 2091 P100 Particulate Filters, preventing cancer-causing silica from reaching your lungs. Since Cattail paid for it, it's your mask, too! Note: these filters will not be effective against gases. Probably not the thing to take to a protest.

My only ask would be that if you use it, return it to and seal the plastic bag, which will extend the life and functionality of the removable pancake-filters.

<img src="assets/salvage.jpg" alt="plastic pallets" width="800">

The next acquisition, to make the recycling job even safer would be safety goggles. Another step in improving the safety of the glass crusher is to get the **shop-vac unit** working (designed to draw away and trap glass dust), or to replace it. If anyone wants to help me with this, I imagine it would be creditable hours. If not, I would compensate you personally in exchange for my own edification and peace of mind.

# this website

I've told several people now how easy it is to make a website like this one. I still believe it to be true. It's free, and can be easily replicated. I could teach you how to do it in half an hour. Minimalist tech stack, open source.

Paying for anything, in my experience, means giving money away for the experience of being gaslit as to how necessary the tool you are paying for really is. Putting a target on your back to be marketed to, suckered into more subscriptions, taught how to interact with a black box, with skills that are not intended to be transferable.

This kind of website, contrawise to the wooly eyes of consumer products, can easily be repurposed, recycled. Once you learn how to catch these digital fish, you can become a vegetarian. Put the pond scum on your garden.

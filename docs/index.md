## Mask

Recycling has a new mask for crushing glass! It's a GVS Elipse SPR549 Elipse Mask Integra P100, preventing cancer-causing silica from reaching your lungs. Check out [its specifications and instructions for use](respirator-equipment.md). Since Cattail paid for it, it's your mask, too! 

> Note: these filters will not be effective against gases. Probably not the thing to take to a protest.

It is currently stored in a plastic bag inside of a banjo case with a "Rolling into Bloomfield" sticker on it. A more permanent home will most likely be found, but the important thing is to keep it out of dust.

The next step in improving the safety of the glass crusher is to get the **shop-vac unit** working (designed to draw away and trap glass dust), or to replace it. If anyone wants to help me with this, I imagine it would be creditable hours. If not, I would compensate you personally in exchange for my own edification and peace of mind.

## this website
